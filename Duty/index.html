Crew duty day and CRP from 2 Gp Ops Manual Part A 7.1.8, C-17 row: 16:00 reporting 0500–1459 local with 1–4 sectors, 14:00 with 5 or more; 14:00 and 12:00 respectively reporting 1500–0459. Minimum CRP 12:00, plus an hour per 4-hour zone change and an hour for each hour CDT exceeds the limit. CDT runs from report to 30 minutes after last chocks-in (7.1.8.4). Crew in is 2:00 before ETD, 2:30 for USA or Theatre (7.1.8.1 fn4), or 5:00 where the ALM is operating (7.1.8.4). Extensions 7.1.8.5, split duty 7.1.8.8.

Not covered here, and still yours to apply: positioning and deadheading (7.1.8.9, 7.1.8.13), delayed departures (7.1.8.15.2), acclimatisation history (7.1.8.2), enduring operations (7.1.8.3.1) and the napping guidance (7.1.8.14).
